VSCodium application icon

Standard stable asset documented in icons/README.md, not Microsoft Visual Studio Code. The external DTD was omitted when rasterizing; the source artwork was unchanged.
The delivered WebP is a proportional raster conversion at a maximum of 96 pixels.
Exact upstream source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VSCodium/vscodium/ca7f7b44b5e5c3faa663f6cdc043998f71936653/icons/stable/codium_cnl.svg
The exact original is also supplied as vscodium-source.svg.txt.
