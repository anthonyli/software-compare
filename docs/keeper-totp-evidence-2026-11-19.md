# Keeper stored-login TOTP

Issue #541. Verified 2026-11-19 from official sources, not an authentication test.

https://help.keeper.io/article/189-how-do-i-set-up-two-factor-codes-for-my-various-websites
https://www.keepersecurity.com/legal/terms-of-use/

Keeper's help documents adding a provider's TOTP configuration to a website/application record, then generating recurring codes for that external service. Not every service accepts TOTP. This is distinct from MFA used to unlock or log into Keeper itself. The usage terms limit Free User accounts to two TOTP-containing records; support does not imply unlimited free use. Existing pricing snapshots remain unchanged. No secrets were entered, credentials transmitted, or account settings changed.
