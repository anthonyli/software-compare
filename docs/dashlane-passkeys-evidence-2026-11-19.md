Official help confirms creating, saving and using passkeys for compatible external services through the browser extension and Android/iPhone apps. The iPhone workflow requires iOS 17 or later. Users must be signed into Dashlane; sites may require additional user verification, with specific biometric/PIN requirements for mobile SSO users. Website, browser and device compatibility varies. Dashlane's own passwordless account login is explicitly not a passkey implementation. No universal platform compatibility, vault-login passkey, pricing, key-export or privacy architecture claim is inferred. Other product fields remain unchanged. No credentials were entered or authentication performed.
