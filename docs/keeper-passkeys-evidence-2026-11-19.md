# Keeper external-service passkeys

Issue #535. Verified 2026-11-19 from official documentation, not an installed-app test.

- https://help.keeper.io/article/268-what-is-a-passkey-and-how-does-keeper-support-them
- https://docs.keeper.io/user-guides/autofill-setup-for-android

Keeper documents creating, storing and using passkeys for compatible external services. KeeperFill handles supported-site requests; mobile use requires configured autofill, Android 14+ or iOS 17+. The Android guide separately lists Android 9+ for general autofill, which is not the passkey minimum. This establishes the passkeys capability, not arbitrary browser/app compatibility or a method of unlocking the Keeper vault. Pricing, sharing, TOTP, emergency access and self-hosting remain unchanged and unverified here. No keys, credentials or accounts were created, entered or transmitted.
