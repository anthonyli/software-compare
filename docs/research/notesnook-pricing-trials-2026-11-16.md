# Notesnook regional pricing and trial scope

Verified 2026-11-16 using official sources:
- https://notesnook.com/pricing: free without card/time limit; standard monthly/yearly trials require a payment method, while prepaid five-year plans have no trial. Tags have plan-specific caps.
- https://notesnook.com/help/plans-and-limits: attachment allowance excludes note text; regional pricing follows the payment-method country. Do not treat a prior SGD view as the global currency or expand the standard-trial claim to Education.
- https://notesnook.com/blog/introducing-new-pricing-plans (2025-10-04): explicitly identifies USD reference prices, consistent with the current pricing page, and monthly allowance reset on the first. Annual references are 19.99/69.99/89.99 USD for Essential/Pro/Believer, not guaranteed local checkout quotes.

The update replaces the old SGD-only assertions in all displayed price fields, adds bounded tag support, and distinguishes monthly attachment uploads from total note storage. Five-year availability is described without inventing a global five-year checkout amount. Do not infer automatic billing on free-tier overages; precise over-limit enforcement was not verified. Other feature claims and release metadata remain unchanged. No account, trial, subscription, payment or runtime test was performed.
