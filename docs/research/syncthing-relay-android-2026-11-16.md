# Syncthing relay privacy and Android distribution

Checked 2026-11-16. Official relaying documentation https://docs.syncthing.net/users/relaying.html states that relay fallback is enabled by default when direct connections fail. End-to-end encrypted content remains encrypted across the relay; the operator can observe IP addresses, device IDs and traffic volume. Absence of hosted cloud storage is not proof that traffic never traverses servers.

The official maintainer announcement https://forum.syncthing.net/t/discontinuing-syncthing-android/23002 (2024-10-20, clarification 2024-10-21) discontinues the official Android app, not all possible Android integrations. https://syncthing.net/downloads/ separately lists base distributions and integrations/community contributions. Keep Android in platforms and mobile partial, without recommending or claiming to have audited a community client. No iOS conclusion added.
