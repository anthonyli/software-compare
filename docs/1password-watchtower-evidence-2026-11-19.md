# 1Password Watchtower evidence

Issue #531. Verified 2026-11-19 from official documentation, not an installed-app or account test.

https://support.1password.com/watchtower/
https://support.1password.com/watchtower-privacy/

Watchtower identifies compromised websites and vulnerable passwords among saved items. Checks and notifications have settings; this is not universal monitoring of all identities or a guarantee of detecting every breach. Website matching uses downloaded information locally. Vulnerable-password checking sends the first five characters of each password hash to Have I Been Pwned and compares returned candidates locally; passwords themselves are not sent. The official privacy page warns about malicious-service inference risks for similar weak passwords and documents opting out. No credentials were entered or transmitted. Existing price snapshots, emergency-access unknown and other metadata remain unchanged.
