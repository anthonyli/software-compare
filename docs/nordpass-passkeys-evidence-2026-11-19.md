# NordPass external-service passkeys

Issue #537. Verified 2026-11-19 using official documentation, not an installed-app or authentication test.

https://support.nordpass.com/hc/en-us/articles/12984678202641-Passkeys-FAQs

The FAQ documents creation and selection of stored passkeys for compatible external websites and applications. Some services require master-password or configured biometric verification. This supports passkeys=yes, not passwordless unlocking of NordPass itself or universal platform compatibility. The FAQ currently rules out passkey import/export to other password managers. Its Google SSO warning explains that a user should not rely solely on credentials in NordPass to access the Google account needed to unlock NordPass. No keys, passwords or accounts were created, entered or shared. Pricing, sharing, TOTP and all other unknown capabilities are unchanged.
